About

One More Night is a first-person survival mystery set in a decaying European apartment block during a citywide blackout.

Every night you have ten minutes outside your own door. Use them. Then come back, write down what you saw, and sleep.

The building will not wait for you to be ready.

Controls

  • W A S D move
  • Mouse look
  • Shift sprint
  • E interact
  • F flashlight
  • Tab inventory
  • J journal
  • Esc pause
  • Q lean / peek
  • C crouch
  • T glance at your watch
  • 1-9 dialogue choices

Advice

  • Lights are not decoration. They are the safest thing in the building.
  • The watch on your wrist is the only clock you can trust. Mostly.
  • Write things down. You will forget. That is part of it.
  • Some clues will not make sense for three more nights.

Built with three.js. All geometry, textures and sound generated procedurally at runtime — no external assets.
